# CP-35 — where a Spring Cloud starter's version lives, and what spells it

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-outgoing-endpoint-addresses-design.md` §5a, plan
Task 1 Step 1. **Probe first**, because the claim is about other people's poms and because
the cause here was guessed wrong three times from the symptom before anyone read either the
poms or `detect/maven.py`.

Re-run it rather than trusting this file:
`bash scripts/probes/cp35_bom_chain/run.sh`. `chain.py`'s output *is* the measurement, and
`run.sh` refuses to print a table whose column header disagrees with the pom it opened.

**Version scope.** Everything below is measured against
**`org.springframework.cloud:spring-cloud-dependencies:2025.1.0`** — the version the opened
pom reported in its own `<version>` element — resolved out of the local Maven repository,
with the four starters `spring-petclinic-api-gateway` declares as the targets. A BOM's
contents are release-scoped: a later `spring-cloud-dependencies` is free to manage these
artifacts somewhere else or spell their versions differently, and the verdicts here bound to
the release that produced the table.

## What the question is, exactly

A module declares a starter with **no version**:

```xml
<dependency>
  <groupId>org.springframework.cloud</groupId>
  <artifactId>spring-cloud-starter-gateway-server-webflux</artifactId>
</dependency>
```

Its parent imports `spring-cloud-dependencies` with `<scope>import</scope>`. So: which pom
finally states a version, how far away is it, and **what is written there** — a literal, a
named property, or something else?

The third part is the one that mattered and the one nobody had looked at.

## Observations

Generated by `scripts/probes/cp35_bom_chain/run.sh`. The column header is the
version the opened pom itself reported in its `<version>` element, not the version
passed in as `BOM` — the harness compares the two and aborts when they disagree.

| dependency | managed by | hops | 2025.1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `spring-cloud-starter-gateway-server-webflux` | `spring-cloud-gateway-dependencies:5.0.0` | 1 | `${project.version}` |
| `spring-cloud-starter-netflix-eureka-client` | `spring-cloud-netflix-dependencies:5.0.0` | 1 | `${project.version}` |
| `spring-cloud-starter-config` | `spring-cloud-config-dependencies:5.0.0` | 1 | `${project.version}` |
| `spring-cloud-starter-circuitbreaker-reactor-resilience4j` | `spring-cloud-circuitbreaker-dependencies:5.0.0` | 1 | `${project.version}` |

`hops` counts `<parent>` and `<scope>import</scope>` edges traversed from the
aggregating BOM. The last column is the RAW version string written at the managing
BOM — unexpanded, which is the finding.

## Observations, read

**1. The chain is short.** One hop from the aggregating BOM to the component BOM that manages
the artifact — two from the module's own parent pom, which imports the aggregator. **There was
no depth problem.** `detect/maven.py` caps its inheritance walk at
`_MAX_INHERITANCE_DEPTH = 16` and already follows both `<parent>` links and
`<scope>import</scope>` entries, so the walk reaches this in a quarter of its budget. A fix
framed as "walk deeper" would have changed nothing.

**2. `spring-cloud-dependencies` manages none of these artifacts itself.** It imports nine
component BOMs and each target is managed one level down, by a different one. `grep -c
spring-cloud-starter-gateway-server-webflux` against the aggregator is **0**.

**3. Every one of the four is spelled `${project.version}`, and that is the finding.**
`project.version` is a Maven BUILT-IN — the version of the pom the element is written in — and
it is **not an entry in any `<properties>` block**. A reader that interpolates only from
`<properties>` therefore resolves nothing for it. Here it means `5.0.0`, the version of the
managing component BOM, and `spring-cloud-starter-gateway-server-webflux:5.0.0` does exist in
the repository.

It is not an edge case in this release: 6 of 8 managed entries in
`spring-cloud-gateway-dependencies` are spelled this way, 5 of 8 in
`spring-cloud-circuitbreaker-dependencies`, 4 of 13 in `spring-cloud-config-dependencies`,
4 of 29 in `spring-cloud-netflix-dependencies`. It is simply how a component BOM versions its
own artifacts.

**4. `chain.py` deliberately does NOT know the built-ins.** Its `expanded()` carries a comment
saying so. The harness reproduces what a `<properties>`-only reader sees, because that reader's
behaviour is what is under measurement — a harness that silently did better than the code would
have hidden the thing it was built to find.

## What this means for the implementation

**The walk is correct and needs no change.** What a reader of these poms must additionally do:

* **define Maven's built-in `project.*` properties per pom**, scoped to the pom being read —
  `${project.version}` means that pom's version and must never be answered by the importing
  module's, which would yield a coordinate that does not exist;
* **report a managed entry whose version cannot be interpolated**, rather than dropping it.
  `_managed_versions` had no `else` on its `if settled is not None:`, so four dependencies
  vanished with no message — which is why the symptom read as "the BOM was never opened" and
  why three wrong causes survived as long as they did.

Neither is about depth, and neither is about which coordinates a catalog lists — that is the
second half of `OI-139` and is measured by the catalog's own skip message rather than here.
