## What the value is (ruling N1)

A read of a key is the deployment's to set, so its value is the expression `${KEY}`. The default
a call carries is the code's fallback and is **not** what the deployment has: the first two rows
of the table are why it is recorded and never published as the value. `getenv(k) or d` carries the
default outside the call; it is read as a branch, never as `d`.
